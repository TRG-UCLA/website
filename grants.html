Current projects

FIRE-WUI: Community Resilience Assessment Against Wildland–Urban Interface Fires

Builds a community-scale resilience assessment framework for WUI fire, using multi-resolution digital twins of the built and natural environment to evaluate exposure, vulnerability, and mitigation options.

National Science Foundation, via Purdue University · 2026–2028

Wildfire Planning Tool for Bidirectional Long-Term Risk Quantification and Management

Develops a planning tool that quantifies wildfire risk in both directions — the risk energy infrastructure poses as an ignition source, and the risk wildfires pose to that infrastructure — to support long-term investment and mitigation decisions.

California Energy Commission · 2025–2029 · with E. Lopez Droguett (PI)

Development of Fuel Model Maps and Prescribed Fire Management Strategies for the Comiteca Tojolabal Plateau, Chiapas, Mexico

Extends the group's AI-based fuel mapping methods to a data-sparse region of southern Mexico and pairs them with prescribed-fire management strategies developed with local partners.

UC Office of the President, via UC Davis · 2025–2027

RAPID: Infrastructure Resilience and Impact Study for Wildfire-Affected Infrastructure in Los Angeles (IRIS-Wildfire)

A rapid-response study of infrastructure performance and recovery following the January 2025 Los Angeles wildfires, documenting impacts while perishable data remains available.

National Science Foundation · 2025–2027

Model Tests for Structures Crossing Highways Under Seismic Loading

Combines physical model testing with computational analysis to characterize the seismic response of structures crossing highways, informing Caltrans design and assessment practice.

Caltrans · 2025–2028

PyroKit: Wildfire Threat, Ignition, and Mitigation Toolkit

Develops a toolkit for assessing wildfire threat and ignition potential and for evaluating mitigation measures, in collaboration with Sandia National Laboratories.

U.S. Department of Energy / NNSA, via Sandia National Laboratories · 2024–2026

A Hybrid Framework of Exascale Simulations, Observations, and Deep Learning for System-Level Clean Energy Resilience and Risk Reduction

Couples exascale earthquake simulation with observational data and deep learning to assess seismic risk to clean-energy systems at regional scale.

U.S. Department of Energy, via Caltech · 2023–2026

Coastal Hazards, Equity, Economic Prosperity, and Resilience (CHEER)

A multi-institution NSF hub studying coastal hurricane hazards; the group's role centers on wind and surge loss modeling for coastal building stock informed by insurance claims data.

National Science Foundation, via University of Delaware · 2022–2027
