Academic family tree

Seventeen recorded generations, as documented in the Mathematics Genealogy Project — from Leipzig in 1622, through Göttingen physics and the Munich and Kyiv schools of mechanics, to UCLA. Two cousin branches join the tree at Thomasius and Kästner, reaching Lagrange and Gauss.
