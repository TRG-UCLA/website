Prospective students

Prospective graduate students with backgrounds in structural engineering, computational mechanics, geotechnical engineering, or applied machine learning are encouraged to get in touch. Please include a CV and a short statement describing which parts of our work interest you and why.

Admission decisions at UCLA are made by the departmental committee rather than by individual faculty, so please apply through the standard graduate program process in addition to reaching out.
